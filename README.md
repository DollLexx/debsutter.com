## Customization Instructions

All placeholder content is clearly marked with `[PLACEHOLDER: ...]` tags. Replace with your actual content:

### Content to Update:

1. **About Section** (`index.html` lines ~55-70)
   - Your 3 bio paragraphs
   - Certifications and training details

2. **Services Section** (`index.html` lines ~80-140)
   - Service descriptions
   - Pricing information
   - Session durations and formats

3. **Testimonials Section** (`index.html` lines ~145-175)
   - Client testimonials (or remove section if not applicable)

4. **FAQ Section** (`index.html` lines ~180-230)
   - Answers to common questions

5. **Contact Section** (`index.html` lines ~235-265)
   - Your actual email address
   - Your actual phone number
   - Your location/service area
   - Your availability/hours

6. **Photo**
   - Add your professional headshot to replace the placeholder
   - Update the `<div class="image-placeholder">` with `<img src="your-photo.jpg" alt="Deb Sutter">`

### Contact Form Setup

The contact form currently shows an alert (placeholder). To make it functional, you can:

**Option 1: Formspree (Easiest - Free tier available)**
1. Sign up at [formspree.io](https://formspree.io)
2. Update the form tag: `<form id="contactForm" action="https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_FORM_ID" method="POST">`

**Option 2: EmailJS (Free tier available)**
1. Sign up at [emailjs.com](https://www.emailjs.com/)
2. Follow their integration guide

**Option 3: Netlify Forms (If hosting on Netlify)**
1. Add `netlify` attribute to form tag
2. Forms automatically work
