About Deb

PSYCH-K® Facilitator & Certified Reiki Master-Teacher

I discovered my own energy in 2010 when I felt it shoot through me, out the top of my head, for the first time in my life at a specific meaningful moment. Energy like that was foreign and of a whole different dimension to me.

I spent the next 14 years trying to understand the what, when, and how of my experience, while dealing with deep emotional challenges of what that energy meant to me and what it meant regarding certain persons in my life. I struggled trying to understand my energy - how to use it and how it could help me.

In 2024, in deep emotional pain and worsening depression, I met a wonderful lady who is a trained Shamanic Healer, Reiki Master-Teacher, a PSYCH-K® facilitator and more. Through energy work (like Reiki) and a single PSYCH-K® session, my emotional pain was gone!! My life began to change for the better.

Experiencing for myself how effective Reiki and PSYCH-K® are, it became my goal to learn these tools to continue the process of making my life better, through healing my body, mind, emotions and spirit. I trained to be a Reiki Master-Teacher. I am currently training in PSYCH-K®, recently having completed the Master level. My PSYCH-K® education will continue in 2026 to Advanced and more!

Healing comes from within - miracles happen - and the right tools help unlock the potential that is already within us. PSYCH-K® and Reiki are tools, the keys to a better life!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Reiki?

Reiki is a holistic healing system originally devised by a Japanese Buddhist monk, Dr. Mikao Usui. It is a healing process that can bring help and comfort to people suffering from a range of conditions, or simply a need to relax and rejuvenate. As a natural form of healing, Reiki can support and enhance your own body's health and well being.

What is PSYCH-K?®

PSYCH-K® is a simple yet powerful way to change the beliefs that shape your life. It helps you tap deep into yourself and reprogram limiting beliegs into ones that support your goals, peace and growth.

Do you offer distance/remote sessions?

For PSYCH-K®, in person or remote sessions are available. Remote sessions are effective and will allow us to work around your busy schedule. For Reiki, at this time, remote sessions are preferable.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions you need is completely up to you. Take it at your own pace, leaving yourself time to feel the effects of the sessions in between. It is possible that only one session will be enough.
