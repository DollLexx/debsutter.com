Where Astrology Fits

Astrology was part of my search for understanding long before I found Reiki or PSYCH-K®. I've studied it independently for years through extensive reading and chart research, with a particular interest in psychological astrology and the work of Liz Greene. It helped me recognize patterns, but for me it was never the whole answer.

I have not completed formal astrological training or certification. I keep astrology supplementary and exploratory while I continue to study and test what I observe.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PSYCH-K®?

PSYCH-K® is a process for working with subconscious beliefs. The work begins with a goal or change you choose, and I facilitate the processes I've been trained to use in relation to that goal.

I've never experienced Reiki. Is that okay?

Yes. You don't need prior experience. Before we begin, I'll explain what we'll be doing and answer your questions. I also won't tell you what you're supposed to feel — your experience is your own.

Do you offer remote sessions?

Remote PSYCH-K® sessions are available, along with in-person sessions in the Bath, PA area. Contact me if you're interested in Reiki so we can discuss the available options.

Is astrology a separate service?

Not at this time. Astrology is a long-term area of independent study that I use as a supplementary perspective. I am continuing to research it rather than promising a stand-alone astrology service.

How many sessions will I need?

There isn't a set number. Your goals, experience, and preferences matter, and you can decide whether and when you want to continue.

How experienced are you working with others?

I use Reiki, PSYCH-K®, and astrology extensively in my own personal inner and life work. I've also practiced Reiki and PSYCH-K® with other people as part of my training. I'm now ready to work with more people and gain experience beyond the training environment.
