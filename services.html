Frequently Asked Questions

What is PSYCH-K®?

PSYCH-K® is a simple yet powerful way to change the beliefs that shape your life. It helps you tap deep into yourself and reprogram limiting beliefs into ones that support your goals, peace, and growth.

How does a session work?

We start by exploring what you want to change in your life. Then, using PSYCH-K® processes, we identify the subconscious beliefs behind what's holding you back and transform them. Each session is at your own pace and fully guided.

Do you offer distance/remote sessions?

Yes! Remote sessions are fully effective and allow us to work around your schedule from wherever you are. In-person sessions are also available in the Bath, PA area.

How many sessions will I need?

That's completely up to you. Take it at your own pace, giving yourself time to feel the effects between sessions. It is possible that even one session will be enough.
