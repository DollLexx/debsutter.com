THE PATH HERE

From experience to exploration.

2010
✦
THE EXPERIENCE

Something happened.

In 2010, something extraordinary happened — I felt energy surge through me and out the top of my head for the first time. I knew the experience was significant to me, but I didn't understand it.

2010–2024
☽
THE SEARCH

Fourteen years of questions.

For the next fourteen years, I tried to make sense of what I had experienced. Astrology became one of the tools I turned to during that search. I read extensively, studied charts, and became particularly interested in psychological astrology and the work of Liz Greene. Astrology helped me recognize patterns and gave me another language for thinking about my experiences, but for me, it wasn't the whole answer.

2024
✧
A NEW FRAMEWORK

Reiki → PSYCH-K®

Then, in 2024, I found Reiki. For the first time, I had a formal practice centered directly on the energy I had spent so many years trying to understand. I continued training through Reiki Master-Teacher. That same year, I discovered PSYCH-K®, which approached things from a different direction: subconscious beliefs and the changes we want to make in our lives. I continued that training as well.

NOW
✦
THE NEXT PART

And now I'm here.

Reiki and PSYCH-K® are the two practices I've formally trained in, and both have become an extensive part of my own personal inner and life work. Astrology remains part of that exploration. I've practiced Reiki and PSYCH-K® with others during training, and now I'm ready to begin working with more people and gaining experience beyond the training environment.

And when I'm not exploring any of that, you might find me in the kitchen crafting a batch of homemade ice cream — one of my favorite ways to bring a little sweetness to everyday life. 🍦
