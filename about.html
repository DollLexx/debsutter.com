About Deb

My work today PSYCH-K® Facilitator Reiki Master-Teacher Independent student of astrology

From experiencing energy to finding ways to work with it.

In 2010, something extraordinary happened — I felt energy surge through me and out the top of my head for the first time. I knew the experience was significant to me, but I didn't understand it.

For the next fourteen years, I tried to make sense of what I had experienced. Astrology became one of the tools I turned to during that search. I read extensively, studied charts, and became particularly interested in psychological astrology and the work of Liz Greene. Astrology helped me recognize patterns and gave me another language for thinking about my experiences, but for me, it wasn't the whole answer.

Then, in 2024, I found Reiki. For the first time, I had a formal practice centered directly on the energy I had spent so many years trying to understand. I began training and continued through Reiki First Degree, Second Degree, Master Practitioner, and Reiki Master-Teacher.

That same year, I discovered PSYCH-K®. It approached things from a different direction: subconscious beliefs and the changes we want to make in our lives. I went on to complete PSYCH-K® Basic, Masters, Advanced, and the Health & Wellbeing Program.

And now I'm here.

Reiki and PSYCH-K® are the two practices I've formally trained in, and both have become an extensive part of my own personal inner and life work. Astrology has also been part of that exploration for many years. I've practiced Reiki and PSYCH-K® with others during my training, and now I'm ready to begin working with more people and gaining experience beyond the training environment.

Astrology is still very much part of how I explore and understand patterns. I've studied it independently for years, but I have not completed formal astrological training or certification. I see it as a supplementary lens rather than a stand-alone promise of answers.

What connects all three for me is the same question that started this journey: what is happening beneath the surface, and what can we learn by looking at it differently?

And when I'm not exploring any of that, you might find me in the kitchen crafting a batch of homemade ice cream — one of my favorite ways to bring a little sweetness to everyday life. 🍦
