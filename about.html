About Deb

PSYCH-K® Facilitator

In 2010, something extraordinary happened — I felt energy surge through me and out the top of my head for the first time. It was unlike anything I'd ever experienced, and it opened a door I couldn't close.

For the next 14 years I searched to understand that experience — exploring what energy meant, how it worked, and how it could serve me. That search led me through many chapters of life, each one quietly pointing me toward something deeper.

In 2024, I found myself at a crossroads — the kind of moment where life asks "what do you want instead?" I met a gifted healer who introduced me to PSYCH-K®. In a single session, something shifted. The heaviness lifted. I walked out different than I walked in.

That's the power of PSYCH-K®. It works at the subconscious level — where beliefs are formed, where patterns live, and where real change begins. Joy, health, confidence, peace, better relationships — these aren't out of reach. They're already within you, waiting for the right key. PSYCH-K® is that key. I trained through Basic, Masters, Advanced, and the Health & Wellbeing Program because I wanted every tool available — for myself, and for you.

When I'm not facilitating sessions, you might find me in the kitchen crafting a batch of homemade ice cream — one of my favorite ways to bring a little sweetness to everyday life. 🍦
